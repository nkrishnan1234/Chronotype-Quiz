You Are A Lion!

This page will tell you everything you need to know about fixing your sleep schedule as a lion.

The Lion Profile

  • The Trait: Natural early risers who wake up full of energy.
  • The Peak: Maximum productivity and focus occur before noon.
  • The Slump: Energy levels drop drastically in the late afternoon.
  • The Goal: Push evening exhaustion back to maintain a social life.

The Ideal Daily Schedule

  • 05:30 AM – 06:00 AM Wake up and seek immediate bright light.
  • 06:00 AM – 07:00 AM Exercise or do high-intensity movement.
  • 07:00 AM – 08:00 AM Eat a high-protein breakfast.
  • 08:00 AM – 12:00 PM Tackle analytical, deep-work tasks.
  • 12:00 PM – 01:00 PM Take a brisk walk outside for afternoon sun.
  • 01:00 PM – 05:00 PM Focus on administrative tasks or meetings.
  • 05:00 PM – 06:00 PM Limit heavy carbohydrate intake at dinner.
  • 09:30 PM – 10:00 PM Dim all lights and wind down for sleep.

Sleep Schedule Fixes

Evening Light

Use bright lights at 06:00 PM to trick the brain into staying awake longer.

Late Exercise

Do light cardio at 05:00 PM to boost evening alertness and delay fatigue.

Strategic Naps

Limit power naps to 20 minutes before 03:00 PM to protect nighttime sleep.
