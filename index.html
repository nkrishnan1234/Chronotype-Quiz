Chronotype Quiz

What is a Chronotype? A chronotype is more than just “early bird” and “night owl”. It is your natural disposition to feel alert or sleepy based on the time of day & night. It is tied to the internal clock in your brain, the circadian rhythm. The Circadian Rhythm guides sleep and other bodily functions. It runs on light exposure. When daylight fades, melatonin is produced, which makes you feel sleepy. Not everyone's rhythm is the same, though. Some produce less Melatonin than others. These differences are caused by genetic differences, which cause different chronotypes. Chronotypes include:

  • Wolf
  • Bear
  • Dolphin
  • Lion
