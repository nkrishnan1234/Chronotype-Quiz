You Are A Dolphin!

This page will tell you everything you need to know about fixing your sleep schedule as a dolphin.

The Dolphin Profile

  • Light Sleeper: Wake up easily from noise or anxiety; light and fragmented rest.
  • Your Energy Peak: Mid-afternoon focus spike between 3 PM and 6 PM.
  • The Slump: Scattered attention span in the morning due to overnight waking.
  • Your Goal: Calm nervous system arousal and reduce bedtime rumination.

The Ideal Daily Schedule

  • 06:30 AM – 07:00 AM Wake up immediately, avoid staying in bed scrolling.
  • 07:30 AM – 08:00 AM Do some stretching.
  • 08:00 AM – 09:00 AM Eat a low-sugar breakfast.
  • 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM Handle logic puzzles or brainstorming tasks.
  • 01:00 PM – 04:00 PM Demanding Work.
  • 06:00 PM – 07:00 PM Physical Activity
  • 07:30 PM – 08:30 PM Eat a carb-supportive dinner to feel more sleepiness.
  • 10:00 PM – 11:30 PM Warm bath, meditation, calm your anxiety.

Sleep Schedule Fixes

No Bed Stress

If you don't sleep in 20 minutes, leave bed. Do not look at the clock.

Brain Dump

Write your tomorrow to-do list at 8 PM to clear mental anxiety.

Cool Room

Keep your bedroom exactly at 65°F (18°C) to lower core temperatures.
