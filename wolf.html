You Are A Wolf!

This page will tell you everything you need to know about fixing your sleep schedule as a wolf.

The Wolf Profile

  • Night Owl: Struggle with mornings, hitting maximum stride late into the day.
  • Your Energy Peak: Prime creative output and energy strike from 5 PM to 9 PM.
  • The Slump: Groggy mornings make early business hours difficult to manage.
  • Your Goal: Ease the morning burden and smoothly shift your sleep window earlier.

The Ideal Daily Schedule

  • 08:30 AM – 09:00 AM Wake up and use your phone or another device (blue light usage is good at this time)
  • 09:00 AM – 10:00 AM Hydrate and do low-intensity work.
  • 11:00 AM – 01:00 PM Do Team work at this time.
  • 01:00 PM – 02:00 PM Eat a late lunch; get sunlight to not feel sleepy.
  • 04:00 PM – 07:00 PM Deep work and problem solving.
  • 07:00 PM – 08:00 PM Perform intense exercise to burn off excess adrenaline.
  • 08:30 PM – 09:30 PM Eat dinner (keep it lighter on fats).
  • 11:00 PM – 12:00 AM No devices, dim environment, read to sleep.

Sleep Schedule Fixes

Morning Light

Get 20 minutes of bright sunlight within 30 minutes of waking up.

Delayed Lunch

Having lunch later helps postpone the afternoon slump in wakefulness.

Digital Sunset

Use no devices after 9PM.
