You Are A Bear!

This page will tell you everything you need to know about fixing your sleep schedule as a bear.

The Bear Profile

  • Solar Rhythm: Your internal clock matches the sunrise and sunset naturally.
  • Your Energy Peak: Deep focus hits best during mid-morning (10 AM to 12 PM).
  • The Slump: A major dip in alertness happens right after lunch around 2 PM.
  • Your Goal: Maintain consistent sleep depth and maximize morning productivity.

The Ideal Daily Schedule

  • 07:00 AM – 07:30 AM Wake up, stretch, and drink water.
  • 07:30 AM – 09:00 AM Get outdoor sunlight and eat breakfast.
  • 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM Tackle complex, high-priority creative tasks.
  • 12:30 PM – 01:30 PM Eat a balanced lunch to lower the risk of afternoon fatigue.
  • 02:00 PM – 04:00 PM Focus on administrative work, emails, or easy tasks.
  • 04:00 PM – 05:00 PM Take a quick screen break or a short active walk.
  • 06:30 PM – 08:00 PM Enjoy dinner around this time.
  • 10:00 PM – 11:00 PM Shut off electronics and get ready for bed.

Sleep Schedule Fixes

Midday Activity

Walk outside for 15 minutes at noon to shake off the afternoon crash.

Protect the Window

Stick to a strict weekend sleep schedule to avoid "social jetlag."

Consistent Caffeine

Delay your first coffee until 10 AM to maximize natural cortisol flow.
